## The Shift

Inference silicon is purpose-built and cheap enough that utilization is no longer the gate to ownership. A few servers with current accelerators, in one room, on one power and cooling plant, can now carry both the transactional processing the business runs and the operation of the facility around it. These workloads are bounded and repetitive, at a scale set by what a business generates in a day. Hyperscale was never sized for this work.

Ownership bounds latency and ensures availability. Moves, adds and changes are not linked to subscriptions. On-site control decisions resolve in milliseconds, not round-trip delays. Operations continue when the internet goes down. And the data is on a machine the business owns, in a room it controls, on a network it operates.

## Two AI domains

On-premises AI divides into two domains. They are not the same workload. Whether they run on one infrastructure is an economic decision, not an architectural one.

### AI IT — runs the business

- Resident model — answers staff and operational queries
- Retrieval — over the operation’s own records and documents
- Transactions and inventory — shrink, out-of-stock, basket composition
- Scheduling and staffing — against traffic, hours and throughput
- Compliance records — retained and queryable on site

### AI OT — runs the infrastructure

- Room sensors — temperature, humidity, airflow, leak, smoke
- Power — UPS, PDU and circuit load, transfer switch
- Cooling — setpoints and airflow
- Cameras — anomaly and access events
- Access control — door locks and badge readers
